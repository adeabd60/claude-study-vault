# Claude route: free chat

For anyone without Projects. Works anywhere you can use Claude, including iPad. The catch: a normal chat forgets everything, so you attach the kit and your module files at the start of each session.

## Keep a "Claude files" folder per module

In OneDrive, next to your lecture files, keep these per module:

```
Term 1/EXAMPLE101 Example Module/
  _module.md
  PROGRESS.md
  Week 1/
    (lecture files)
    Week 1 - Pack.md
    Week 1 - Tutorial Breakdown.md
```

Keep `TEACHING-METHOD.md`, your `MY-SETUP.md` and `templates/` in `Uni/Year 2/_system/`.

## Each session

1. New chat. Attach `TEACHING-METHOD.md`, `MY-SETUP.md`, the module's `_module.md` and `PROGRESS.md`, and:
   - for `prep`: the week's lecture files, last week's Pack, and the templates;
   - for `teach` / `coach`: this week's Pack and Tutorial Breakdown (plus the lecture files if they fit).
2. First message:

   > Follow TEACHING-METHOD.md and MY-SETUP.md. My Claude route is Free chat. Prep week 1 of EXAMPLE101.

3. At the end, save what Claude hands back: working files (`.md`) into the OneDrive folder above, notes into your notes app.

## Tips

- **Usage limits are tight on the free plan.** Prep and teach in separate chats; keep sessions focused.
- Big slide decks use a lot of your allowance. Attach the transcript and tutorial sheet first; add slides if Claude asks.
- Save working files as plain `.md` (on Windows: Notepad › Save as › "All files", name ending `.md`; on iPad: the OneDrive or Files app, or ask Claude for a downloadable file).
